Is DragonERP suitable for my business?

The product overview is aimed at growing trade, e-commerce and service businesses with teams of 5–50 in Australia and New Zealand. Your required modules, data and integrations still need an individual assessment.

Does shipment tracking update automatically?

Automatic freight connections have not been confirmed for the proposed visibility pilot. It can begin with an assigned person checking the source and recording progress. Any integration needs its own assessment.

Can I migrate from my current system?

Yes. Start with stock only and check it against Xero, or trial-import supported Xero, MYOB or SAP export files and reconcile balances. Coverage and the cutover plan are agreed after checking your data.

What will it cost?

Cloud subscriptions are A$48.88 a month or A$488 a year per five users (excl. GST). An on-premises licence is A$1,888 one-off for 20 users (excl. GST). Implementation and migration are quoted after a walk-through.

How do I subscribe or buy?

Choose a monthly, yearly or on-premises plan, complete the opening details, then review and email the prepared request. We confirm your order, payment or trial eligibility, and opening arrangements. Only monthly subscriptions include a three-month trial; yearly subscriptions and on-premises licences require confirmed payment.
