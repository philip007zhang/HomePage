“Where’s the order?”
A better place to start.

For businesses sourcing goods from China, customer orders and freight updates can sit in different places. One proposed pilot brings the relevant information into the sales workflow.

Proposed pilot scope—not a live customer case study. Availability and suitability are to be confirmed.

  1. 01
    Connect the records

    Associate customer orders with the relevant shipment or shipments.

  2. 02
    Make uncertainty visible

    Show the latest known status, its source, and when it was checked. Keep unknown arrival dates clearly marked.

  3. 03
    Give follow-up an owner

    Identify who updates progress and what needs checking next. Manual updates can be a practical starting point.

A few useful questions

Before we
get started.

Is DragonERP suitable for my business?

The product overview is aimed at growing trade, e-commerce and service businesses with teams of 5–50 in Australia and New Zealand. Your required modules, data and integrations still need an individual assessment.

Does shipment tracking update automatically?

Automatic freight connections have not been confirmed for the proposed visibility pilot. It can begin with an assigned person checking the source and recording progress. Any integration needs its own assessment.

Can I migrate from my current system?

The supplied overview describes imports of supported Xero, MYOB and SAP export files. Start with a trial import and balance reconciliation; coverage and the cutover plan are agreed after checking the data.

What will it cost?

Pricing is to be confirmed for your agreed scope. Setup, pilot support, hosting and any ongoing services are clarified before work begins.
